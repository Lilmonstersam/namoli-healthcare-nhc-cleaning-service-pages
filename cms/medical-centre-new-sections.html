How Often Should a Medical Centre Be Cleaned?

Most practices need routine cleaning on each operating day. The right schedule depends on patient traffic, clinical activities and your practice’s infection control risk assessment.

During the Day

When daytime cleaning is part of your programme, our team can respond to spills and visible soiling promptly. We coordinate between-patient cleaning with your clinical protocols so everyone knows who is responsible.

Each Operating Day

Most medical centres need routine cleaning each day they operate. Our visits cover high-touch surfaces, bathrooms, consultation areas, reception and floors, with extra attention where your risk assessment requires it.

Weekly or Periodic

We can schedule lower-touch surfaces, internal glass and detailed floor care to suit how your centre is used. Periodic work complements everyday cleaning.

We review the schedule with you as services, patient numbers or infection risks change. Read our RACGP accreditation cleaning and infection prevention guide.
